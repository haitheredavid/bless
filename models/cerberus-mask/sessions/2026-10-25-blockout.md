# 2026-10-25 blockout

## Goal
head proxy + sketch guides + SDF blockout + variant grid

## Steps
- Step 1 `head-proxy`: generic adult head in Ref from SDF blobs: 170 × 219 × 378 mm, pupils at z=260, neck on z=0. Asset libraries are off in the addon, so it isn't a downloaded scan.
- Found that Points→SDF leaves a narrow band too thin for SDF Fillet/Mean: 8.9k non-manifold edges, 165 islands. Offset(+r/−r) closing also tears. Fix in `GN_SDFBlob`: Grid→Mesh→Mesh-to-SDF (band 6) before fillet. 0 non-manifold, ~0.2 s at 3 mm voxels.
- Step 2 `heads-blockout`: `GN_CerberusMask_Heads` on `Model/Heads`.
  - Inputs: `Control/CTL_Head/CTL_Head_{Cranium,Snout,Ears,Jaw}` Bezier curves (radius = thickness, Alt+S).
  - Center head from those parts; a yawed/scaled side head on a generated neck, mirrored.
  - Inputs: Snout Length (stretch about the face plane), Ear Size, Jaw Open (about the hinge), Full Mask (center jaw).
  - Result: 420 × 232 × 235 mm, manifold.
- Live grid (base, tight, flared, long-neck, snarl, full-skull, chunky). Restacked 4×2 for one look, then cleared.
- Step 3 `sketch-guides`: `front.svg`/`top.svg` imported into `Ref/Ref_Sketches` at 1:1 mm, aligned on the pupils.
- Step 5 `asym-heads`: the mirror is replaced by per-side placement.
  - New inputs: Left/Right Pitch and Tilt (about the neck root), Right Yaw/Drop/Back Offset, Cranium Scale.
  - The symmetric base is unchanged by the refactor: 71,706 faces, same bounds.
  - Variants: `asym` (−15% volume, 515 × 262 × 351 mm) and `asym-lean` (−28%, 537 × 296 × 382 mm). Both manifold.
- Step 6 `collar-heads`: collar mode.
  - Proxy is now a bust down to mid-chest (486 wide over the deltoids). Eye height raised 260 → 420: existing CTL/proxy curves moved +160, edits kept; proxy neck extended into the chest.
  - New `Control/CTL_Collar/CTL_Collar_Yoke` (a loop over the shoulders).
  - `Side From Collar`: neck roots sit on shoulder anchors (Shoulder Width/Drop/Forward) and the necks rise down-and-back from the skull. Pose knobs still apply.
  - The face mask is a separate island from the yoke and side heads.
  - Results: `collar` 663 × 346 × 394 mm; `collar-asym` 581 × 435 × 392 mm, 3 islands, manifold.
  - Temple-mode base is unchanged by the refactor: 71,706 faces.
- Steps 7–9, refinement in the chosen direction.
  - collar-asym is now `[base]`. The old setups are variants `temple` / `temple-asym`.
  - Necks are Bezier curves: `Neck Curve` 60 (swan arch), `Neck Taper` 0.6.
  - Head curves replaced with dog-skull shapes: braincase + crest, cheekbones, nasal bridge, solid mandibles + lower fangs, three-curve broad ears.
  - Yoke thicker, with shoulder caps.
  - Fillet 12 / Smooth 3 is needed so thin parts blend (0.8 s per evaluation).
  - Result: 575 × 429 × 405 mm, 174k faces, 2 islands (face mask; yoke + side heads), `blend check` clean.
- Steps 10–11, mask shell and vision.
  - The center head is now the wearable mask: SDF difference of its inside (Wall 2.4), the face proxy grown by Face Clear 6, everything behind Mask Back 40, and the cutters.
  - Cutters are curves tagged `cut` that ride along with their head:
    - center vision cones (`CTL_Head_Cuts`);
    - side-head sockets (`CTL_Head_CutsSide`);
    - nostrils, stretched with the snout (`CTL_Head_CutsSnout`).
  - Muzzle root radius 28 → 22: the sightline from the pupils (x ±31) passes beside the muzzle instead of through it.
  - The mask drops loose pieces under 25% of its biggest (freed cheekbone bits). Center Scale 1.15.
  - FOV per eye (`out/steps/fov_check.py`): out 104° L / 34° R (the right side head blocks 34–44°), inward 7°, up 16°, down 86°.
  - Trade-off: that FOV opens the eye and cheek area of the face; the mask is brow + muzzle + ears.
  - `blend check`: 573 × 429 × 430 mm, 176k faces, 2 pieces, clean.
  - Look tool: `distance` is unusable in mm files (reported). Frame close-ups with an invisible vertex-only box as `target`.
- Step 12 `eye-visor` (option B): `Visor` object.
  - Same tree with `Output Visor` on: a 1.2 mm plate on the mask's inside (face proxy + Face Clear), trimmed to the vision cutters + 8 mm glue margin, with a hex grille (3.0 mm holes, 0.9 mm bars).
  - 0.5 mm voxels, eye region only. Result: 109 × 24 × 47 mm, 2.9 cm³. It is **two pieces**, one grille lens per eye (`checks.py` caught it; I first reported one piece). `blend check` clean.
  - Fixes along the way:
    - Rebuilding `GN_SDFBlob` with reset=True emptied its interface and left HeadProxy with 0 faces, so the face cut silently did nothing. Now only its nodes are cleared.
    - SDF Intersect gave half the region (565 cm³) instead of the thin plate, so the trim is a Manifold mesh boolean.
  - Visor evaluation takes ~12 s; MCP calls time out at 30 s, so probe one evaluation per call.

- Step 13 `build-pipeline`: the build moved out of MCP scratch scripts.
  - `builders/10_proxy.py` bakes HeadProxy to a static mesh and re-bakes only when the proxy blob curves change.
  - `20_heads.py`: `Work/Rig` (`GN_CerberusMask_Rig`, posed tagged curves) feeds `Model/Mask`, `Body` and `Visor`, each its own group with only its inputs.
  - `30_guides.py` imports the sketches once. `checks.py` runs the requirement checks (pieces, closed meshes, FOV, weight).
  - `just build cerberus-mask` rebuilds headless in ~51 s.
  - kit changes:
    - `gn.tree(reset=True)` keeps the interface; added `gn.prune` and `gn.panel`.
    - `params.apply` / `missing` cover Model + Work. The first headless build exposed that the Rig in Work got no params.
    - The variant grid copies rig dependencies.
  - Checks report:
    - weight ~7.1 kg if printed solid in PETG; Body is ~5,550 cm³, Mask ~63 cm³.
    - FOV through the mask: 120° out/down, 17° up.
    - The side heads block the left eye from 104° and the right from 34°.

## Outcome
- The blockout is live and parametric; `params.toml` is synced to the built inputs.
- Not built yet: Flesh, Horn Length, strap slots, Body shell. (Side From Collar, Face Clear and Wall were built in steps 6 and 10–11.)
- Speckle: step 13 published as version `a4b62fc7cd` (model `26b42d6761`).
- Grid read:
  - **long-neck** and **flared** read as three heads.
  - In base/tight the center dome (r 66) swallows the side heads: it reads as a helmet/turtle.
  - Side jaws (U-curve) show as open rings.
  - `chunky` barely differs. Voxel size alone doesn't make facets; it would need decimate/low-poly.
- Blender addon protocol 13 < 15: run `just addon` and restart Blender when convenient.
- Builders are not promoted yet: the logic lives in `builders/blob_geo.py`, and steps are in `log/`.
- Gotchas:
  - Rebuilding `GN_SDFBlob` (its source hash changed) unlinked the group node in `GN_CerberusMask_Proxy`, as in the known gn-patterns case: the proxy was empty until step 01 re-ran. Builder order at wrap: proxy and heads both rebuild their trees after `blob_group()`.
  - A tree rebuilt with reset=True zeroes modifier inputs that aren't in params: `Eye Height` dropped to 0 and the side heads fell to the floor. `Eye Height` is now in `[base]`.
- `blend check`: Heads 421 × 232 × 234 mm, 71.7k faces, no issues.

# art-dealers-logo: print pack

150 mm two-colour desk block of the Art Dealers Galeria logo, printed as one part on the P1S: an open black tray (3 mm floor, 4 mm rim, 15 mm tall) with the white logo standing inside up to the rim top; the 'Dealers' letters are knocked out through the white box to the black floor.

Generated by `blend print-pack` (2026-10-25T18:23:40-04:00), spec `print-spec/1`. Units **mm** (1 Blender unit
= 1 mm). Material PLA. Bed 256 mm.

- `parts.blend`: only the printable parts, modifiers applied, each on the bed (z=0), laid
  out without overlaps. Open it as the printing session's model.
- `stl/`: one STL per part (mm).
- `manifest.json`: this information, machine-readable.

Orientation: as modelled, flat on the floor underside. Needs AMS: the black rim rises past the white start height (z = Base Thickness), so a single filament swap at a layer does not work.

# witch-frame: print pack

3D-printable shadow-box frame for a 9x12in witch illustration: concave bat-wing sides with flared corner tips, a face sloping from a thin outer edge into a deep sightwell, and a rabbet with snap-in flex prongs for the art stack.

Generated by `blend print-pack` (2026-10-17T10:09:34-04:00), spec `print-spec/1`. Units **mm** (1 Blender unit
= 1 mm). Material PETG (Insignia, black). Bed 256 mm.

- `parts.blend`: only the printable parts, modifiers applied, each on the bed (z=0), laid
  out without overlaps. Open it as the printing session's model.
- `stl/`: one STL per part (mm).
- `manifest.json`: this information, machine-readable.

Orientation: as modelled, back face down; the rails turned about z (rotate rules) so each lies along the bed's diagonal: a 356 mm side needs a 251.4 mm square, a 279 mm side 197.6. One long + one short side nest on a plate (long along the anti-diagonal, clear of the front-left exclusion zone; short in a corner triangle). Bambu's auto-arrange can't place the long rails (4.6 mm slack; 2026-10-16 test slice: 'Rail_R partly inside'), so place the two rail plates by hand until the pack carries locked plates. Keys flipped (deep end on the bed, so first-layer squish stays off the end that passes the pocket's lip). Prong slots and spline sockets have 45 deg gable roofs, key pockets a hip roof: no supports inside them. Slice with supports on, build plate only (normal(auto), 0.2 mm top gap) for the flat 3.7 mm glazing lip at z 9 (2026-10-11 coupon).

## Hardware

## Assembly
- Print the Coupon pack first and check the corner (see its notes).
- Dry-fit on a flat surface, face down. Push Spline_TR and Spline_BR into Rail_R's miter sockets, Spline_TL and Spline_BL into Rail_L's (each sits 5 mm deep; a drop of CA or epoxy if loose; wood glue does not bond PETG).
- Close Rail_T onto Rail_R over Spline_TR, and Rail_B onto Rail_L over Spline_BL, pushing straight across each miter.
- Close the two L halves along the diagonal: TL and BR meet over their splines at the same time.
- Press a Key into each corner pocket from the back until both snap arms click under the lip. To release one, push its arm toward the slot through the back with a small screwdriver.
- Drop in glazing (2 mm), the art on its 3 mm backboard, then push each prong's tongue out into its rabbet slot with the bump on the board until its barbs click into the slot's end pocket; the arm springs the stack against the lip. A firm pull on the arm releases one to change the art (45 deg barbs).
- Snap the Hanger's two tongues into the slots at the top rail's centre (keyhole entry toward the art, plate flat on the board) until both click.
- Hang: set the nail with its head ~1.8 mm off the wall. Offer the frame so the head enters the keyhole's round end, then let it drop ~8 mm onto the slot.
